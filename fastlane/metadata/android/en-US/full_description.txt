Budget Notes is a private, local-only Android app for budget notes and a personal card vault.

• Notes with Add / Deduct line items and a live total
• Cards tab for payment and ID cards with front/back photos
• Fingerprint / device lock (or app PIN if needed)
• Databases and card photos encrypted on device
• Copy card fields for checkout; clipboard clears automatically
• No accounts and no network permission — data stays on your phone

Photos are stored only inside the app (not in your gallery). Card numbers and other fields are entered manually.

Open source. Uninstalling the app removes all data.
